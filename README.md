# Be-There-First

**Helping students learn how to support a friend who is struggling.**

[**Visit the Website →**](https://averageprogrammer78.github.io/Be-There-First/)

Be There First is an interactive mental health education website that helps students learn how to recognize when a friend may be struggling, respond with empathy, and connect them with appropriate support.

Built for the **Congressional App Challenge**, the project uses realistic, branching scenarios to help students practice peer support in a safe, educational environment.

## About the Project

Knowing that a friend is struggling and knowing what to do about it are two different things.

Be There First bridges that gap through interactive storytelling, practical guidance, and immediate feedback. Instead of simply reading about mental health, students practice navigating difficult conversations and learn how their choices can affect someone who needs support.

## Features

- **Interactive Scenarios:** Explore realistic situations involving panic, social withdrawal, grief, and concerning statements from a friend.
- **Branching Choices:** Make decisions throughout each story and experience different outcomes.
- **Immediate Feedback:** Understand why certain responses are more supportive and what could be done differently.
- **Psychological First Aid:** Learn the *Look, Listen, Link* framework for recognizing distress, listening with empathy, and connecting people with appropriate support.
- **Gamified Learning:** Engage with interactive progress indicators, trust scores, streaks, and badges.
- **Practical Guidance:** Learn when to involve a trusted adult or seek additional help.

## The Scenarios

1. **Panic at the Locker** — Responding to a friend experiencing panic.
2. **A Friend Who Goes Quiet** — Recognizing changes in a friend's behavior and reaching out supportively.
3. **After a Loss** — Supporting a friend experiencing grief.
4. **When a Friend Says Something Worrying** — Responding to concerning statements and recognizing when additional support is needed.

## Technology

- **HTML5** — Page structure and interactive scenarios.
- **CSS3** — Layout, visual design, animations, and responsive styling.
- **JavaScript** — Branching narratives, interactive choices, feedback, and gamification.

The project uses a lightweight, browser-based architecture without requiring a backend server.

## Mission

Be There First aims to make peer support more approachable by helping students build the knowledge and confidence to respond when someone they know is struggling.

Sometimes, knowing how to listen, what to say, and when to seek help can make a meaningful difference.

## Important Disclaimer

Be There First is an educational resource, not a substitute for professional mental health care, diagnosis, or treatment. It does not provide crisis intervention services.

If someone may be in immediate danger, contact emergency services or an appropriate crisis support service. Students should involve a trusted adult when a friend's safety is a concern.
