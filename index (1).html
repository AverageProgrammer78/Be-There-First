What PFA Is

Psychological first aid is basic, practical support for someone who just went through something upsetting. It works like pressure on a cut until help arrives. It does not diagnose or treat. It helps a person feel safe, heard, and connected to the right help. Friends often notice before adults do, and most of us were never taught what to say.

Where It Comes From

What It Can Do

Help someone feel less alone, steady them in the moment, and get them to real help sooner. The WHO recommends it, and anyone can learn it.

What It Can't

Replace therapy or professional care. Rigorous trials of PFA are limited, so it is described as evidence-informed practice, not a treatment.

Why I Made This

In 2023, 42.4% of U.S. adults reported personally knowing someone who had died by suicide, according to the CDC. Behind that statistic are real people, families, and communities affected by loss. I created Be There First because recognizing when someone is struggling and knowing how to respond can be difficult, especially for students. My goal is to help young people listen without judgment, recognize signs of distress, and connect friends with appropriate support when it matters most.
